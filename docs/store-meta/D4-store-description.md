```
🏊 Pool's day — 오늘 어디서 자유수영 할까?

동네 자유수영 수영장을 지도에서 찾고, 시간표를 확인하고,
내 수영 일정을 친구와 함께 잡을 수 있는 앱이에요.

골라봐요. 요일·레인 수·요금·시설(키즈풀·다이빙풀·주차 등) 모두
```

## English — Full Description (App Store 필수)

```
🏊 Pool's day — Find your free-swim pool today

Discover free-swim pools nearby, check schedules, and plan your
sessions with friends.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

✨ Features

🗺️ Map view at a glance
See nearby free-swim pools as markers. Filter by day, lane count,
fee, and facilities (kids pool, diving pool, parking, etc).

🕐 Free-swim schedules
Day-by-day and seasonal schedules in one screen. Weekday/weekend
pricing, lane count, pool length, depth — plus operator notices.

📅 Manage your swim plans
Pick a time slot and save it as a plan. Conflicts are blocked
automatically; mark past plans as "completed" to keep a log.

👫 Together with friends
Add friends and invite them to your plans. See friends' upcoming
swims gently on the map. Privacy levels: private / friends-only /
public — your choice.

💌 Contribute to the community
Suggest new pools or report schedule changes. Operators review
and apply quickly.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔒 Minimal data collection

Most profile info (gender, birthday, swim history) stays on your
device. Only essentials (nickname, avatar, notifications) are
stored on our server. Location data is kept in memory only and
discarded immediately — never stored on the server.

💙 No ads, donation-supported

Pool's day runs without ads or paid features, supported by warm
donations from swimmers like you.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📩 Contact

Reach out via [Settings > Help Center > FAQ > Email us].

See you in the water. 🏊‍♀️ — Pool's day
```
